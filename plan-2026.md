# Data Engineering for the AI Era

This roadmap provides a structured, hands-on path to mastering the fundamentals of Data Engineering, DataOps, and AI-integrated systems using resources current for 2025–2026.

### Structure - A Unified Pipeline 

- **Days 1-30: The Pipe** (Data Engineering & Contracts).
- **Days 31-60: The Process** (DataOps, FinOps, & Semantic Layer).
- **Days 61-90: The Intelligence** (ML, RAGOps, & Trust).

| Sprint | Phase | Focus & Fundamental Deep Dive | Primary "Accessible" Lab Tools |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Days 1-10** | **Data Engineering** | Partitioning, Storage Formats (Parquet/Avro), & Compute vs. Storage. | **Python & DuckDB** |
| **Days 11-20** | **DataOps** | Idempotency, DAG Theory, & State Management. | **Dagster & GitHub Actions** |
| **Days 21-30** | **Data Contracts** | Schema Evolution (Forward/Backward compatibility) & Producers vs. Consumers. | **Pydantic & YAML** |
| **Days 31-40** | **Semantic Layer** | Dimensional Modeling (Star Schema) & Metrics as Code. | **dbt & Metricflow** |
| **Days 41-50** | **Data FinOps** | Tokenomics, Query Optimization, & Resource Allocation. | **Cloud Billing APIs / OpenCost** |
| **Days 51-60** | **DS & ML Logic** | Statistical Significance, Bias, & Feature Vectorization. | **Pandas & Scikit-Learn** |
| **Days 61-70** | **RAG & AI Eng** | Semantic Search, Vector Embeddings, & Context Windows. | **Pinecone & LangChain** |
| **Days 71-80** | **Trust & Security** | Differential Privacy, PII Detection, & Adversarial Prompts. | **WhyLabs & Redaction Scripts** |
| **Days 81-90** | **Data as a Product** | Metadata Strategy, Data Discovery, & Stakeholder UX. | **Streamlit & Evidence.dev** |

## Phase 1: Data Engineering (The Infrastructure)

**Focus:** The movement, storage, and transformation of data.

*   **Core Concepts:** SQL for Analytics, Python (Data Classes/Decorators), Lakehouse Architecture (Iceberg/Delta Lake), and Streaming.
*   **YouTube Resources:**
    *   [DataTalksClub: Data Engineering Zoomcamp 2026](https://www.youtube.com/@DataTalksClub)
    *   [Seattle Data Guy: Modern Data Stack 2026 Architecture](https://www.youtube.com/@seattledataguy)
*   **O'Reilly / Books:**
    *   *Fundamentals of Data Engineering, 2nd Edition* (Reis & Housley, 2025).
    *   *Architecture of the Data Lakehouse* (Bill Inmon, 2024/2025 update).
*   **Hands-on:** Build a pipeline using **DuckDB** and **Python** to ingest a public API dataset into a structured Parquet-based Lakehouse.

## Phase 9: Data as a Product (The Value)

**Focus:** Transforming pipelines into "Data Products" that are discoverable, usable, and valuable to both humans and AI agents.

*   **Core Concepts:**
    *   **Product Thinking:** Moving from "Project" (completing a task) to "Product" (supporting a long-term user need).
    *   **Data Portability:** Making data easily accessible across different platforms (e.g., Lakehouse to AI Agent).
    *   **User Experience (UX) for Data:** Ensuring metadata, documentation, and samples are clear for both humans and LLMs.
    *   **Data Mesh Principles:** Domain-driven design where the team closest to the data owns its quality.

*   **YouTube Resources:**
    *   [Starburst: Adopting a Data Product Mindset](https://www.youtube.com/@StarburstData)
    *   [The Data Chief: Strategy & Data Hero Mindset](https://www.youtube.com/@ThoughtSpot)

*   **O'Reilly / Books:**
    *   *Data Mesh* (Zhamak Dehghani).
    *   *The Data Hero* (Malcom Hawker, 2025/2026).
    *   *Data Products For Dummies* (Starburst/Accenture Edition).

*   **Hands-on:**
    *   **Build a "Data Product Portal":** Create a simple README or a lightweight **Streamlit** app that acts as a "Storefront" for your Phase 1–8 work.
    *   **The Test:** Give an AI agent access to your data without explaining anything. If it can successfully generate a report using your Semantic Layer (Phase 4), you have built a **Data Product**.
